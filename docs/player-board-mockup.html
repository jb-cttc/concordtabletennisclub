Player Board

A second window the desk opens and drags onto the gym TV. It shows which group each player is in, whether they're signed in, and whether their payment is recorded, and it follows every sign-in, move and score on the desk. Try the three layouts below with the round robin and both Equalizer formats, and use the desk buttons to watch changes come through.

A · Carousel

Two groups at a time, one slides in

Your idea as described. Every few seconds the next group slides in from the right and the left one slides out. Names are big enough to read from across the gym. When the desk changes a group that's off screen, that group slides in next, so nobody waits a full lap to see a move.

Best for: round robin nights with 4–7 groups.

B · Wall

Every group at once, nothing moves

All groups on one screen, with smaller type. Nobody waits for their group to come round, but it's harder to read from the back of the gym once there are more than 6 groups. After printing, each group shrinks to a standings table.

Best for: a big TV near the desk, or small nights.

C · Spotlight + names

One group large, everyone listed A–Z

One group at a time on the left. On the right is every player A–Z with their group and a payment check. It answers "am I signed up, and where?" without waiting. After printing, the list shows each player's W-L, or where they stand in the Equalizer.

Best for: busy sign-in, and Equalizer afternoons.

How it would hook into the desk

  • A Player board button in the desk toolbar opens this as a second window. You drag it onto the TV (the desk laptop's second screen) and press Full screen.
  • The desk draws the board itself, so every sign-in, group move, payment and score shows up within a second, even before Save to Sheets. It makes no Google calls and reads nothing from the Sheet, so it doesn't count against the club's daily Google limits. I checked this on /dev today without saving anything: the desk opened its own window and updated it.
  • Stages follow the desk: Signing in until the sheets print, then scores. Round robin groups show the printed sheet's grid, filling in as the desk enters results. Equalizer groups show each match's head start and the Finals bracket. Double Elimination shows up next, the Main Draw, the Survivor Trail and who's still unbeaten.
  • It covers the "TV board" in the Equalizer plan (issue #104, step 5): on the tables, up next, group tables and a mini bracket. Both formats would share one board.
  • Payment shows only recorded (green check) or not yet (empty box), never how someone paid. Play-pass holders show as recorded. The ticker announces sign-ins, moves and results, but never payments.

Things to decide

  1. Layout. One for both stages, or a mix (for example, the carousel while signing in and the spotlight once scores start)?
  2. Equalizer before the sheets print. The desk redraws the random groups with every sign-in, so this concept shows the field A–Z instead of draft groups that keep reshuffling. Groups appear once the sheets print.
  3. When it switches to scores: when the sheets print (as here), or at the first result.
  4. Payment checks: on by default? (There's a switch above.) Keep the "1st visit" welcome chip?
  5. Look: Night or Paper depends on the TV and the gym lights. It's worth trying both on the real screen.
  6. A TV not connected to the desk laptop (a smart TV or a stick) would need the board as its own Google page, reloading saved data every so often. That uses Google quota and shows only saved changes, so it would be a second step if wanted.

Everything here runs on the club's real Equalizer rules (draw, head starts, groups, brackets) and round robin standings rules. Players, ratings, payments and results are made up.
