About the Club

The Concord Table Tennis Club has been in continuous operation since 1972. We are affiliated with USA Table Tennis (USATT), the national governing body for the sport.

The club's mission is to provide a friendly yet competitive playing space, with good lighting, quality tables, nets, and balls for everyone who wants to enjoy the sport. Beginners, novice, intermediate, and advanced players are all welcome.

We are a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, volunteer run, and open to the public. The club is located at Walnut Creek Christian Academy in Walnut Creek and holds play four times a week: Mondays, Wednesdays, Fridays, and Saturdays. Walk-ins are always welcome, and loaner paddles are available.

How a Club Night Runs

Volunteers run our club nights with a few simple tools built by members. The Club Night Guide explains them in plain steps:

  • Signing up to open and close the gym, and texting ahead to say you are coming
  • The round robin desk: the laptop where volunteers sign players in, form groups by rating and enter scores
  • Score sheets, the standings on the projector, and the results and ratings posted to this site
  • Coaching lessons on Friday and Saturday nights

You do not need to read it to play. It is there for the curious, for new volunteers, and for developers, who can open the technical notes under each step. The examples use made-up names; no member information is shown.

Read the Club Night Guide. Ideas for improving it are welcome: tell an officer, or open an issue on GitHub, where the code is shared.
