Opening the meeting app…

Sign in to open the meeting

The executive committee's meeting app opens only for the club's Google account. This device is not signed in to it yet, or this browser keeps Google out of this page (Safari on a phone usually does). The button opens the app on its own, where Google can sign you in.

Sign in with the club's Google account

Signed in to more than one Google account on this device? Google opens the app with the account that was signed in first, and may say You need access. If Google's page offers a choice of account, pick the club's. Otherwise open a private window, come back to this page, and sign in with the club's account there.

This browser could not keep the screen on: set the computer not to sleep while the board is up.
