How this works

  • Players are grouped by rating, the way the round robin groups them. Each zone shows two tables; groups are 5 or 6 players.
  • Playing tonight counts everyone signed in at the front desk and everyone who texted the club today to say they are coming.
  • Likely marks regulars who, going by their recent Mondays and Wednesdays, come about 9 nights in 10. Expected is our forecast: about as many as usually come on this night of the week, shared out by rating according to who has been coming lately.
  • Only counts are shown here, never names.
  • Sign up in person between 6:45 and 7:15 PM; play starts at 7:30. Running late? Text (925) 238-3505 before 7 PM and your spot will be held.
