Coaching

Private and group lessons are available from coaches who are members of the club. Lessons are for-fee and arranged directly with each coach. Lessons are offered on Fridays 7 to 10 PM and Saturdays 3 to 6 PM, as 25 or 50 minute lessons. Use Request a lesson to pick an open time, then reply YES to the email we send you; a lesson is confirmed only when the coach accepts. To cancel or move a lesson, tap your time and confirm the same way. On the schedule, coaches appear by first initial (Coach O for Olaf) and students only as Student A, Student B and so on. Coaches on the club's list can use the same button, tap I am a coach, pick their initial, choose their times and confirm them by replying YES to our email (or text, for coaches with a mobile number on the list). No account is needed. You can also contact a coach directly by email or find them at the facility on club nights.
