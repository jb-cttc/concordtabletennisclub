Coaching

Loading the coaching schedule…

Private and group lessons are taught by club member coaches.

  • When: Fridays 7 to 10 PM and Saturdays 3 to 6 PM. Lessons are 25 or 50 minutes.
  • Fees: Each coach sets their own lesson fee, paid directly to the coach. The regular session fee is paid at the front desk.
  • Booking: Select Request a lesson, choose an open time, and reply YES to the confirmation email. The lesson is booked once the coach accepts.
  • Changes: To cancel or reschedule, select your lesson time and confirm by email.
  • Privacy: The public schedule lists coaches and students by first name only (Coach Olaf, Student Sam). Coaches see a student's full name with each request. Once a lesson is confirmed, the student and coach each receive the other's name, email, and mobile number (if given).
  • Coaches: Select Request a lesson, then I am a coach, to post available times. Confirm by replying YES to the email or text.
  • Direct contact: You may also email a coach below or speak with them at the club.
