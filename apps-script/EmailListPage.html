Sending a club update

  1. In Gmail, address the email To the club’s own address.
  2. Press Copy all for Bcc and paste into Bcc. Never To or Cc: everyone would see everyone else’s address.
  3. Copy the list fresh each time you send. An old copy would still email people who have since unsubscribed.
  4. End the email with this line, so anyone can stop the emails:
