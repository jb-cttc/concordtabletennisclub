Coach management

A coach gets the green ✓ on the coaching page when all three steps are done: they have answered one of the coaching app's emails from the address on file, texted the club number from the mobile on file, and published both lesson rates. A new address or number counts only once the coach answers from it. Coaches see the same steps, and what to do, when they pick their name on the coaching page. Email addresses come from the club's coach list (coaching.html) and change by a reviewed pull request; mobile numbers are set here.
