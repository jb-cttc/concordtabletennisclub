What the desk would have done

Each completed card payment on Clover is matched to a player only when the evidence points to exactly one: a card or Clover customer matched before, the name on the card (or Clover customer) exactly matching one player or saved alias, or the same last name with a first name or initial only one active player fits. The amount must then be that player's fee (Junior $5, Member $10, Visitor $15), or the fees of a payer and the players a NameLinks "pays_for" row says they pay for.

"Your record" compares with the payment chosen by hand at the desk that night. Nights are worked through oldest first, and the desk learns as you would have taught it: a matched card is remembered, and a night left with one unmatched payment and one Credit player at the same amount links that card to that player.

Refunds, voids, declines and cash entered on Clover are listed but never matched. Card details are the brand and last four digits only.
