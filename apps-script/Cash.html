Range totals

Fees per night: Junior $5, Member $10, Visitor $15. A Zeffy monthly play pass covers the night, so pass holders are counted but owe $0. Expected is what was recorded as paid by Cash, Venmo, Zelle or Credit; outstanding is what unpaid players still owe.

Status is each player's status today: the desk keeps no status history, so someone who became a member later shows at the member fee on earlier nights too. Archived players count as visitors.

Round robin players come from the saved roster and Open Play from the desk's Open Play list (Friday and Saturday nights are Open Play only). "Paid, not on the sheet" is a payment recorded for someone on neither list, kept so no payment is lost.
