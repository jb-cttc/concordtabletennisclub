The Equalizer

Round robin results

Same club, same players, a new way to play: a head start in every game makes every match a fair fight, and you have two lives before you're out.

Saturdays · sign up 2:45–3:15 PM · play starts 3:30 · same fee as a round robin

  1. The drawEveryone who signs up goes into a random draw. No seeds: you could meet anyone.
  2. 50
    Your head startEvery full 40 rating points between you is 1 point at the start of every game, up to 8.
  3. Two livesLose once and you play on along the Survivor Trail. Lose twice and your day is done.
  4. +8
    8 points on the lineEvery match is worth 8 rating points, whoever you play: win and gain 8, lose and give 8.
