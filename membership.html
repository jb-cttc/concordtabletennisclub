The club is open to everyone, members and non-members alike. Membership is $60 per year and covers all family members living at the same address. Membership is prorated by quarter.

Monthly Play Pass (Members Only)

Members can also sign up for a $65/month auto-pay plan via credit card, which covers unlimited drop-in nights for that month. This is a good deal if you play more than 6 times a month.

The check-in desk tracks active passes automatically as Zeffy processes each payment, so there is nothing to show when you arrive.

How to join

Download the membership form, fill it out, and bring it along with your membership fee on your next visit. Fees can be paid by cash, check, Zelle, or Venmo at the front desk.

Help open and close the club

Every club night needs volunteers: a desk organizer, a money collector and table setup to open, and at least two people to put the tables away at close. Select Staff sign-up to see which nights need help, pick as many nights and positions as you like, and reply YES to the confirmation email. The schedule shows first names only.

Club organizers: Cash flow shows each night's takings from the check-in desk. It needs the club's Google account.

Questions? Contact us at the front desk during the posted play times on the Home tab, or use the contact form.
